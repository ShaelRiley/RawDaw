# Five source Song Packs

These `.prdepack` archives contain 123 actual source-derived assets and one saved
estimated source drum measure each. The first three are CC BY 3.0. Parachute is
Shael Riley's user-authorized Suno source, with its separate rights declaration
preserved. Shop Vac uses Jonathan Coulton's official stems under CC BY-NC 3.0,
with attribution and noncommercial restrictions. Every pack retains credit and
processing changes in its provenance.

Each `_a.prdeproj` and `_b.prdeproj` is a new four-bar arrangement using that pack.
`parachute_full.prdeproj` is the complete 112-bar, **2:42.91** demo at 165 BPM,
with a recurring chorus hook and contrasting sections. Its companion
`.arrangement.json` documents the form. Open it in the desktop or read
[the full demo score](../docs/PARACHUTE_DEMO.md).
Run `make source-demo` from the repository root to export and verify all nine.

| Pack | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reach_out | B natural minor, 113 BPM | D major, 126 BPM |
| where_you_are_now | C natural minor, 100 BPM | C Dorian, 118 BPM |
| auld_lang_syne_bossa | F major, 130 BPM | D natural minor, 110 BPM |
| parachute | B-flat natural minor, 165 BPM | B-flat harmonic minor, 132 BPM |
| shop_vac | E major, 150 BPM | C-sharp natural minor, 128 BPM |

All five retain their original recorded archives. Raw Daw 0.4.0 supplies a
complete source-derived instrument bank around each one: MIDI 0–127 for lead
and bass, 420 root-position chords plus lazy inversions, and the offered kit
slots. Open hats and toms are transformed from recorded percussion rather than
substitute synthesizers. Listening and complete source-alignment checks remain
open. Read [source credits and preparation](../docs/SONG_PACKS.md).
